## The title

**`React UI Components Without Tailwind`** (36 chars).

`react ui components` leads because it is the only term in the category that is both
proven and winnable: **KD 0**, 400/mo of proven traffic, and two separate pages hold it at
#1 and #2. Every larger term in the same cluster is out of title range on the KD rule -
`react components` 70, `react ui` 74, `ui library` 76, `react library` 84,
`ui component library` 59, `react component library` 50 - so they live in the description,
the lede, the FAQ and `keywords` instead. That is expected for a front door and is not a
failure of the page; those terms are won later, on brand, or not at all.

The second half is the differentiator with the best evidence behind it. The
tailwind-alternative cluster is **KD 1** and carries ~850/mo of proven traffic across
`alternatives to tailwind css` (350), `tailwind alternatives` (200), `tailwind alternative`
(150), `tailwind css alternatives` (90), `alternatives to tailwind` (60), `tailwind
competitors` (40) and `tailwindcss alternatives` (40) - and four separate pages rank for it
(flyonui, kombai, tinyhref, an r/sveltejs thread), none of them a component library. Zyncat
UI is the answer to that query in a way shadcn, Magic UI, Aceternity, daisyUI, Flowbite,
HyperUI, Preline and FlyonUI structurally are not: all of them require Tailwind.

"Zyncat" is not in the title. It has no search demand, the H1 and the URL already carry the
name, and 36 characters spent on the category is worth more than 7 spent on a brand nobody
is looking for yet.

  this category actually is - and none of it is available to anyone but the brand that owns
  it. It is why the page is titled after the category and not after itself.
- **`design system` (3500/mo, KD 69) and `design systems` (2100/mo).** Proven, on-topic in

  Wikipedia, `designsystems.com`, `designsystems.surf`, `uxpin.com/studio/blog/
best-design-system-examples`. Nobody on that SERP is looking for an npm package. The phrase
  stays in the description because the product genuinely is one; `react design system`
  (KD 33, 100/mo, three ranking pages) is the version that carries package intent, and that
  is the one in `keywords`. `design system examples`, `popular design systems`, `list of
design systems`, `design system libraries`, `open source design systems` are the same

## Notes on what the FAQ may claim

Every FAQ answer is grounded in `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `package.json`,
`src/tokens/*.css` or `apps/docs/components/pages/introduction.tsx`:

- "More than 40 components" - `apps/docs/content/registry.tsx` has 48 doc entries, 4 of
  which are guides (`introduction`, `installation`, `theming`, `mcp`), leaving 44 component
  pages; `package.json` exposes 54 export subpaths.
- "Each on its own subpath" and `import { Button } from '@zyncat/ui/button'` - the exports
  map, plus README's "There is no barrel entry, deliberately."
