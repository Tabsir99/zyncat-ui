# tiktok - keyword research

Primary: **tiktok ui** (KD 0, >100 bucket, **600/mo proven** - careerfoundry #1, figma.com/community `tiktok-ui-screens` #2, figma `tiktok-app-ui-kit` #1, magnific #3)
Secondary: **tiktok clone** (KD 0, 300/mo - codecanyon #1, appscrip #2, github SashenJayathilaka #2), **tiktok interface** (KD 0, 300/mo - figma #1, magnific #2), **tiktok mockup** (KD 0, 250/mo - figma #1, admockups #3), **tiktok ui overlay** (KD 0, 250/mo - figma #1, rightblogger #4)

Candidates: 635 unique ideas + 501 unique questions across 36 seeds. Past the >=20/mo
traffic gate: 39. Shipped: 14.

## The searcher, and the two crowds standing in front of them

This is a replica page. The person it is for is a developer or designer rebuilding
TikTok's post surface - a clone project, a tutorial, a portfolio piece, a design
reference. TikTok has the most hostile keyword neighbourhood of any page in this docs
set: the head term `tiktok` alone is `MoreThanTenMillion` volume and every inch of it
belongs to somebody else.

**The consumer and the creator.** This is the bulk of the raw pull and none of it is
ours. `tiktok downloader`, `tiktok video downloader`, `download tiktok video without
watermark`, `tiktok viewer`, `tiktok coins`, `tiktok recharge`, `tiktok shop`,
`tiktok login` - all `MoreThanHundredThousand`. Then the creator half: `best time to
post on tiktok` (>1K, and eight more variants of the same question), `how to make a
viral tiktok video` (>1K), `tiktok video editor` (>1K), `tiktok template` (>1K, which
turns out to mean **CapCut** templates, not UI templates), `how to reset tiktok feed`,
`how to refresh tiktok feed`, `tiktok not working`, `tiktok profile viewer` (>10K).
Seeding `tiktok app` returned almost nothing but `how to watch tiktok without app`
(>1K) in eleven phrasings. Seeding `tiktok react` returned zero developers: every row
is `how to react to a video on tiktok`, `tiktok react images`, `duet and react`. The
word "react" is TikTok's own feature name, which makes the obvious `tiktok react` seed
worthless here in a way it is not for any other page in this set.

**The ban and divestiture news cycle.** `tiktok news` (>10K), `europe accuses tiktok
design pushes change`, `tiktok design news`, `tiktok api news`, `tiktok api rate limits
impact data platforms`, `short video platform news today`, `when is tiktok coming back
on app store`. Half the `tiktok design` seed is this story wearing a design word.
Rejected wholesale - the page has a shelf life measured in years and this cluster's is
measured in weeks.

So bare `tiktok` is unusable, and unlike the Instagram and Facebook pages there is no
`tiktok feed` fallback either: that seed is `how to reset tiktok feed`, `embed tiktok
feed on website` and shared-feed help articles.

## Why `tiktok ui` leads

It is the one head term in this subject that is both TikTok-specific and owned by our
crowd, and the proof is unusually clean. KD **0**, and **600/mo** of traffic sitting on
it across four separate ranking pages: careerfoundry's "TikTok UI Explained" essay at
#1, `figma.com/community/file/.../tiktok-ui-screens` at #2 (752/mo on the page overall),
`figma.com/community/file/.../tiktok-app-ui-kit` at #1 (138/mo), magnific's vector pack
at #3 (380/mo). Every single result in that top ten is a static asset or an essay -
Figma community files, Adobe Stock, Vecteezy, a Reddit thread about Spotify. Nothing in
it is a working component, and a live React replica is a better answer than any of them.

`Clone` is the second word because the developer shape is real, free and already
mapped: `tiktok clone` is KD 0 with 150-300/mo across six ranking pages, and the SERP
splits cleanly into two halves we can beat - `github.com/kirkwat/tiktok` (#1, 73/mo, a
React Native clone) and `github.com/SashenJayathilaka/TIK-TOK-Clone` (#2, 99/mo,
Next.js) on one side, and agency landing pages selling turnkey apps (code-brew 51/mo,
techbuilder 97/mo, appscrip 36/mo) on the other. The GitHub repos are whole
applications with a backend; the agency pages are a sales pitch. A single documented
component that draws the surface correctly is the thing neither of them is.

Pairing them as `TikTok UI Clone` also collapses an ambiguity in the title itself:
`tiktok clone` in consumer hands means the **clone effect** (`how to clone yourself on
tiktok`, `what is the clone effect on tiktok`, `how to do the clone effect on tiktok` -
a large slice of that seed's question rows). Nobody looking for the video effect clicks
"TikTok UI Clone - React Post Component".

## The mockup and overlay clusters - kept, and where the two lines are

`tiktok mockup` (KD 0, 250/mo) and `tiktok ui overlay` (KD 0, 250/mo) are the
format-angle cluster and they are proven money. The same Figma file,
`tiktok-ui-mockup-fully-customizable`, ranks **#1 for both** and earns 603/mo;
admockups (209/mo), inbeat (160/mo) and mediamodifier (120/mo) sit alongside it, and
rightblogger's safe-zone post takes 224/mo. A designer searching `tiktok mockup` wants
a TikTok post that looks real, and a component that draws one at the platform's own
metrics is an honest answer - better than a PSD, because it does not age out of date.
Kept, and answered in FAQ 5.

**Line one is the file format.** `tiktok overlay` (800/mo), `tiktok overlay png`
(450/mo), `tiktok safe zone` (900/mo) and `tiktok safe zone template` (300/mo) are the
four highest-traffic terms this research found, and all four are rejected. The intent
is a transparent PNG or a .mov to drag onto a timeline in CapCut, Premiere or OBS -
envato's page ranking for them is a _video template_ listing. Somebody searching
`tiktok overlay png` who lands on a React component page bounces, and no FAQ answer
fixes that. `tiktok ui overlay` is kept because its own SERP is the Figma UI file, not
the PNG packs, and FAQ 5 states the boundary in the same breath as it claims the term.

**Line two is the word _fake_.** `fake tiktok` (450/mo), `fake tiktok post` (90/mo),
`fake tiktok post generator` (20/mo), `fake tiktok maker` (20/mo) and `fake tiktok
video` (20/mo) all clear the gate and none of them ship. This follows the line already
drawn on the facebook-feed page: the intent behind the word is prank and deception -
prankshit.com, zeoob, fauxpost - and attaching a design system's component page to it
misrepresents what this is. `tiktok post generator` (150/mo) **is** kept, because its
SERP is genuinely mixed: postiz, Canva's AI TikTok generator, alkai and taskade sit in
it alongside the prank sites, and a component that renders a post is an honest answer
to the non-prank half.

## The collision with the sibling replica pages

`instagram-feed` and `facebook-feed` already hold the shared social-UI vocabulary, so
none of it appears here. `social media feed ui`, `social feed ui`, `feed ui`, `news
feed ui`, `activity feed ui`, `instagram feed ui`, `instagram reels ui`, `facebook
reels ui` and `youtube shorts ui` are all in the kept table at `placed in: none`.

That split costs nothing measurable, because none of it has proof anyway. The `feed ui`
seed returned twenty rows and **every one** is `LessThanHundred`; `serp video feed ui`,
`serp short video app ui` and `serp vertical video feed` all returned **zero entries**,
which is the signal that no page ranks for them with enough traffic to measure. The
same is true of the developer phrasings the brief asked for: `serp tiktok clone react`
returned zero entries and `kd tiktok clone react` returned a **null** difficulty. They
are in the copy where they read naturally and nowhere in the keywords array, because
nothing proves them.

## Rejected clusters

Each of these dominated the raw pull. They are listed so the next agent does not
re-litigate them.

**1. Consumer intent - watching, downloading, accounts.** Rejected wholesale. The
entire `tiktok` seed (`tiktok downloader`, `tiktok video downloader`, `tiktok viewer`,
`tiktok coins`, `tiktok recharge`, `tiktok shop`, `tiktok login`, `tiktok leggings`,
`monedas de tiktok`, `descargar videos de tiktok` - all 100K+), the `tiktok video`
seed (`download tiktok video`, `download tiktok video without watermark`, `tiktok video
saver`, `save tiktok video` - 1K-10K), the `tiktok app` seed (`how to watch tiktok
without app` in eleven phrasings, `tiktok app not working`, `how to clear tiktok app
cache`), the `tiktok profile` seed (`tiktok profile viewer` >10K, `tiktok profile
picture downloader`), and the `tiktok feed` seed (`how to reset tiktok feed`, `why is
my tiktok feed so bad`). None of it is a UI question.

**2. Creator and growth intent.** Rejected. `best time to post on tiktok` (100K+) and
nineteen restatements of it, `how to make a viral tiktok video`, `how to go viral`,
`tiktok hashtag generator`, `tiktok name generator`, `invisible username tiktok
generator`, `how to get more followers on tiktok generator`, `tiktok video editor`,
`ai tiktok generator`, `how much should i charge for a tiktok post`. Adjacent
vocabulary, opposite job.

**3. The US ban and divestiture news cycle.** Rejected on shelf life. `tiktok news`
(>10K), `when is tiktok coming back on app store`, `europe accuses tiktok design pushes
change` (which contaminated more than half of the `tiktok design` seed in six
permutations), `tiktok api news today`, `tiktok api rate limits impact data platforms`,
`short video platform news today`, `meta vibes ai video feed` (which is what the `video
feed` seed mostly returned).

**4. Video-editor overlay assets - the largest traffic in the whole pull.** Rejected on
artifact type. `tiktok overlay` (800/mo), `tiktok overlay png` (450/mo), `tiktok safe
zone` (900/mo), `tiktok safe zone template` (300/mo), `tik tok overlay` (70/mo),
`tiktok ui overlay png` (70/mo), `tiktok video overlay` (40/mo), `transparent tiktok
overlay`, `obs tiktok overlay`, `tiktok overlay stream`. The searcher wants a
transparent PNG or a .mov to drop onto a timeline - envato's ranking page is a video
template listing, rightblogger's is a free PNG download. A React component cannot be
dragged into CapCut. `tiktok ui overlay` alone is kept, because its SERP is the Figma UI
file rather than the PNG packs.

**5. Prank and fake-post generators.** Rejected on the same line the facebook-feed page
drew. `fake tiktok` (450/mo), `fake tiktok post` (90/mo), `fake tik tok` (70/mo), `fake
tiktok generator` (30/mo), `fake tiktok post generator` (20/mo), `fake tiktok maker`
(20/mo), `fake tiktok video` (20/mo), `fake tiktok template` (20/mo). Every one clears
the traffic gate; none ships. The ranking pages are prankshit.com, zeoob and fauxpost.
`tiktok post generator` is kept instead, because postiz, Canva and taskade share that
SERP and the intent there is not deception.

**6. Ad-creative mockups.** Rejected on capability. `tiktok ad template` (200/mo),
`tiktok ad generator` (150/mo), `tiktok ad mockup` (40/mo), admockups.com and inbeat's
ad-mockup generator. A TikTok ad preview needs the Sponsored label and the CTA button
bar, and the component has neither - claiming the term would be inventing a capability.

**7. Turnkey clone scripts.** Rejected on artifact type. `tiktok clone script` (150/mo,
codecanyon #1 and a Medium sales post at #3), `tiktok clone app development`, `tiktok
clone app development cost india 2025`, `tiktok clone app developer`, `tiktok clone app
development service`. The searcher is buying a finished product with a backend, video
transcoding and an admin panel. We ship one component.

**8. The clone _effect_.** Rejected as a homograph. `how to clone yourself on tiktok`,
`how to clone yourself in a video tiktok`, `what is the clone effect on tiktok`, `how to
do the clone effect on tiktok`, `tiktok clone effect`, `how to make a clone video on
tiktok`. This is a large slice of the `tiktok clone` question rows and it is a video
effect, not software. Handled by the title: `TikTok UI Clone - React Post Component`
cannot be mistaken for it.

**9. The `tiktok react` seed, entirely.** Rejected. Twenty rows, zero developers: `how
