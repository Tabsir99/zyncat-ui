# otp-field - keyword research

Primary: otp screen (KD null, 200/mo proven, dribbble.com/tags/otp-screen #2)
Secondary: otp verification ui, react otp input, otp form, otp input

Candidates: 890 unique | past the >=20 traffic gate: 24 in subject | shipped: 15

## The finding that shaped the page

Two separate things are true at once here, and they pull in opposite directions.

**One.** Every keyword in this space with real volume belongs to somebody else's
subject. `otp` itself is 44,000-56,000 monthly traffic and the three pages that
earn it are a Reddit thread about texting slang (30.1K/mo), Wikipedia's security
article (16.6K/mo) and MDN's security article (17.7K/mo). Not one is a UI
component, and one of them is not even software.

**Two.** The developer cluster that _is_ our subject has almost no measurable
volume at all, and no incumbent. `input-otp.rodz.dev` - the docs site for the
library shadcn/ui wraps - earns **0/mo**. shadcn's own Input OTP page earns
**10/mo**. Ant Design's, Chakra's, Preline's, Flowbite's, HeroUI's, PrimeReact's
and Material Tailwind's OTP and pin pages all earn **0/mo**. LogRocket's
tutorial: 0. GeeksforGeeks': 0. Apple's own one-time-code autofill doc: 0.

So the page cannot win on the head term and there is nothing to beat on the
descriptive one. The volume that _is_ both real and ours turned out to be in a
third place entirely: **Dribbble's OTP tag pages**, which earn 62/mo and 54/mo
answering `otp screen` (200), `otp verification ui` (90), `otp screen ui` (70)
and `otp verification screen` (70). Those are people asking what an OTP entry
screen should look like. A live, interactive, copyable React field answers that
better than a static design shot does, and the entry is weighted accordingly.

## The homograph: `otp` is rejected as a head term, permanently

This is the `odometer` case again, only worse - `odometer` had one competing
sense, `otp` has four:

1. **Fandom slang**, "one true pairing". `otp meaning` 84,000/mo, `what does otp
mean` 32,000/mo, `otp meaning text` 26,000/mo, plus `otp buddy` (2,000) and
   `otp x` (2,500), which are shipping tools. The #1 result for bare `otp` is a
   Reddit thread in r/study.
2. **The security concept.** Wikipedia's `One-time_password` (#2, 16.6K/mo) and
   MDN's OTP page (#3, 17.7K/mo), plus OneLogin, iProov and Okta.
3. **OTP Bank**, the Hungarian retail bank, at #6 on the bare SERP.
4. **The OTP gene**, at #8 - genecards.org ranks on the front page for it.

The generator confirms it from the other side: seeding `otp` returns twenty
ideas and twenty questions, and every single one is slang or consumer support -
`what does otp mean snapchat`, `what does otp mean in text from a girl`,
`sleeping otp meaning`, `amazon otp`, `otp bot`. Not one is a component query.

Seeding `otp form` is the same story in miniature: `sassa otp form` (South
African social grants), `hdb otp form` (Singapore housing), `nc otp form`, `id
card otp form`, and twelve question rows that are all `what is the full form of
otp`. Only `otp form` itself survives, and only because web.dev's SMS OTP _form_
article ranks #1 for it.

**Do not re-litigate this.** Bare `otp` is not winnable, is not wanted, and the
clicks would bounce. It appears in the title only as the middle token of `React
OTP Input`, bracketed on both sides, exactly as `odometer` is.

## Title

`React OTP Input & Verification Screen UI` - 40 chars.

`React` leads so the fandom reading is foreclosed before a crawler or a human
reaches `OTP`, and `OTP` is never bare: `React OTP Input` is contiguous, which
picks up the 60-traffic dev term and the 20-traffic `otp input` head inside it,
and `Verification Screen UI` reaches the 70+70 design pair. Rejected:
`OTP Input Component` (leads bare - slang risk), `React OTP Input Component`
(gives up the whole design cluster, which is 3x the traffic), `React
Verification Code Input` (`verification code input` has no measurable traffic
anywhere).

## Notes for whoever picks this up next

- **The demand is India-weighted.** `react-otp-kit.vercel.app` takes 78.5% of
  its traffic from India, `input-otp.rodz.dev` splits US/Indonesia. Ahrefs' free
  traffic checker is global-only - `--country in` returns nothing - so there is
  no way to size the Indian side of this cluster with these tools. If the page
  underperforms its US numbers, that is where the rest of it is.
- **`kwr traffic` on a URL returns `-` when the client is rate-limited and a
  real integer (including `0`) when it is not.** Do not read `-` as zero. Half a
  round of this research had to be re-run after the mint pool was drained by
  back-to-back batches; batches of 6-10 with the default cache stayed healthy.
- **The free tool caps `topKeywords` and `topPages` at 5 regardless of
  `--limit`.** A niche keyword can only be proven through a page small enough
  that the keyword is in its top five - which is why every number above comes
  from a micro-site or a single deep URL, never from a domain-level call on
  shadcn, Ant Design or Mantine.
