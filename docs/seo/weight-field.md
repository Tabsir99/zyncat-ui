## The subject, and the homograph that hides it

Nobody types "weight field". The component is a display headline whose letters ramp their variable-font
`wght` axis under the pointer, so the demand sits under `variable font`, `font animation` and
`font weight`, not under the component's name. `weight` on its own is body weight, weight loss and
dumbbells; it was never seeded, and every retained row carries `font`, `type` or `typography` with it.

The finding that decides the page: **the entire variable-font SERP is explanatory**. Google Fonts,
MDN, web.dev, Wikipedia, Monotype and Adobe all answer "what is a variable font"; v-fonts.com is a
catalogue of faces. Not one result is a working interactive demo you can install. `variable font animation`
and `variable font hover effect` return _no SERP rows at all_, and `kd` scores both at 0. The one
component page anywhere near this vocabulary is magicui's text-animate at #7 for `font animation`
(KD 11, 200 mo) - and it does not animate a font, it animates text position. That is the opening.

### The `none-sibling` rows

`morphing-text` already claims `text animation`, `animated text`, `animated headline`,
`hero text animation`, `rotating text animation` **and**, in its own keyword array,
`kinetic typography` and `typography animation`. `typing-lines` claims `typing animation` and
`typewriter effect`. Those thirteen rows are proven demand this page could plausibly rank for -
`kinetic typography` is 800 mo at KD 15 and `animated typography` is 150 mo at KD 3 - and they are
deliberately left unspent so the two pages do not cannibalise one another. The split that keeps them
apart is clean: morphing-text and typing-lines own **text** in motion; this page owns the **font**
in motion. Every term kept here carries `font`, `weight`, `wght`, `stroke` or `hover`.

design type`, `what type of research design is a survey`. Plus the typeface-drawing craft
  (`type design software`, `type design school`, `swiss type design`), which is about drawing fonts,
