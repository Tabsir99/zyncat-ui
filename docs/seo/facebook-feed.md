# facebook-feed - keyword research

Primary: facebook ui (KD 1, >100, ranks for: dribbble.com/tags/facebook-ui #6 / 150 mo)
Secondary: facebook clone (KD 0), facebook news feed (KD 30, >100), news feed ui (KD 0, mobbin #6 / 20 mo), facebook post ui, facebook post mockup (KD 2, socialbee #1 / 250 mo)

## The searcher, and the two crowds standing in front of them

This is a replica page. The person it is for is a developer or designer rebuilding
Facebook's news feed surface - a clone project, a tutorial, a portfolio piece, a design
reference. Two much larger crowds share their vocabulary, and both had to be routed
around rather than served.

**The end user.** `generator facebook feed` is almost entirely them: `facebook feed not
loading` (>100), `why is my facebook feed all ads` (>100), `how to reset facebook feed`
(>100), `remove stories from facebook feed`. Same for `facebook reels` (`facebook reels
downloader`, monetization, watch history), `facebook story` (`facebook story viewer
anonymous`, screenshot notifications) and `facebook profile` (`how to lock facebook
profile`, >10K). This is the bulk of the raw pull and none of it is ours.

**The page owner.** `embed facebook feed` (Hard), `how to embed facebook feed on website`
(>100), `add facebook feed to wordpress`, `facebook feed plugin`, `social feed widget`,
`wordpress social feed plugin`. That is Smash Balloon's market. We are a React component,
not an embed service, and saying otherwise would be a lie the FAQ would then have to
defend.

So bare `facebook feed` - despite being the slug - is not the term to build on. It is
KD-Hard, and both crowds above own its SERP.

## Why `facebook ui` leads instead

It is the one head term in this subject that is _both_ Facebook-specific and owned by our
crowd: KD **1**, >100 volume, and dribbble.com/tags/facebook-ui earns 150/mo from it at
position 6 - a tag page of Facebook interface designs, which is exactly the intent. The
`facebook ui` SERP is Figma community files (`facebook-ui-screens`, `facebook-app-ui`),
Dribbble, Envato graphic templates and Magnific vectors. Every one of those is a static
asset. A live React replica is a better answer than any of them, and nothing in the top
ten is a component.

`Clone` is the second word because the developer shape is real and free: `facebook clone`
is KD 0, and the `X clone react` pattern is proven across the neighbours - `twitter clone
react`, `instagram clone react native`, `netflix clone react`, `slack clone react`,
`notion clone react`. The `facebook clone` SERP is split - github.com/topics/facebook-clone
(#2), a Udemy "Building a Facebook Clone" course (#5), r/FlutterDev (#3) on the developer
side; techlicious and a local-news piece about **cloned accounts** on the other. Pairing it
as `Facebook UI Clone` collapses that ambiguity in the title itself: nobody searching how
to report a cloned account clicks a page called "Facebook UI Clone - React News Feed Post".

`News Feed` takes the rest of the title because it is the Facebook-specific name for the
surface and it is the ground the sibling page cannot take. `facebook news feed` is KD 30 at

> 100 - the highest bar in the shortlist, which is why it sits in the title, description,
> lede and FAQ rather than leading. `news feed ui` and `newsfeed ui` are KD 0 and mobbin's
> news-feed screen page earns 20-30/mo from them at #6 and #9; a component page beats a
> screenshot gallery on that query.

## The split with instagram-feed

The sibling page is claiming the shared social-feed vocabulary (`social media feed react`,
`social feed ui`, `social feed component`), so none of it appears in this title,
description or keyword list. That is a deliberate cost: `social media feed ui`, `social
feed ui`, `feed ui`, `activity feed ui` and `react feed component` are all in the kept
table with `placed in: none` or keywords-only. The competitor data says the split is the
right way round - zeoob.com's Instagram generator page earns 4,626/mo while its Facebook
page earns 179/mo. Instagram is the bigger half of that shared vocabulary; Facebook's own
half is `news feed`, `post card` and the reels/story surfaces, and this page takes those.

## The mockup cluster - kept, and where the line is

`facebook post mockup` (KD 2) and `facebook mockup` (KD 1, >100) are the format-angle
cluster, and they are proven money: socialbee's Facebook post mockup generator ranks #1 for
`facebook post generator` (1,000/mo) and `facebook post mockup` (250/mo); mediamodifier and
trymypost sit alongside it. A designer searching `facebook post mockup` wants a Facebook
post that looks real, and a component that draws one at the platform's real metrics is an
honest answer - better than a PSD, because it does not age. So the cluster is kept and
answered in the FAQ.

The line is drawn at the word _fake_. `fake facebook post` is KD 3 with 800/mo of proven
traffic across four ranking pages, and it is the single highest-traffic term this research
found. It is not in the keywords list and it is not in the copy. The intent behind it is
prank and deception - prankshit.com, prankmenot.com, a Play Store "prank tweet" app - and
attaching a design system's component page to it would misrepresent what this is. The FAQ
answers the honest half of the query ("can I use it as a Facebook post mockup?") and states
the boundary in the same breath.

## Rejected clusters

- **End-user Facebook support.** `facebook feed not loading`, `why is my facebook feed all
ads`, `how to reset facebook feed`, `facebook feed not updating`, `remove stories from
facebook feed`, `how to set newsfeed to most recent`, `facebook newsfeed down`. The
  largest single block in the pull and the whole of the `facebook feed` head term's SERP.
  Different person entirely - they want their own account fixed.
- **Page-owner embed widgets.** `embed facebook feed`, `how to embed facebook feed on
website` (>100), `add facebook feed to wordpress`, `facebook feed plugin`, `facebook feed
embed`, `social feed widget`, `wordpress social feed plugin`, `social feed aggregator`,
  `embed social feed on website`, `how to add social media feed to squarespace / wix /
shopify`. Smash Balloon's market. This component renders the chrome of a post you supply;
  it fetches nothing from Facebook, so claiming this cluster would be dishonest.
- **Fake/prank post generators.** `fake facebook post` (KD 3, 800/mo proven), `fake
facebook post generator`, `fake facebook post maker`, `fake facebook account`, `fake
facebook profile`, `fake post`, `fake social media post`. The highest-traffic cluster
  found and deliberately not taken - see the section above. The adjacent honest half
  (`facebook post mockup`, `facebook mockup`) is kept instead.
- **Cloned-account security.** `how to report a facebook clone`, `why do hackers clone
facebook accounts`, `how to clone someone facebook account`, `how to stop facebook clone`,
  `facebook clone apk`, `facebook clone mod apk`, `facebook clone script nulled`. Rides in
  on `facebook clone` and is the reason the title says `Facebook UI Clone`, not
  `Facebook Clone`.
- **Offshore clone-script sales.** `facebook clone script`, `facebook clone app development
company`, `on-demand facebook clone app development`, and the whole `clone script` seed
  (`binance clone script`, `airbnb clone script`, `onlyfans clone script`). Agency
  lead-gen for turnkey apps with backends. We ship a UI component, not a product.
- **Reels/story consumer queries.** `facebook reels downloader`, `facebook reels
monetization`, `how to reset facebook reels algorithm`, `facebook story viewer anonymous`,
  `does facebook notify story screenshots`, `how to delete facebook story`. The surface
  names are ours; these questions about them are not.
- **`react facebook` = React the library.** `does facebook use react`, `did facebook create
react`, `github facebook react stars`, `facebook react native`, `companies using react
facebook netflix airbnb`. Plus the reaction-emoji half (`angry facebook react`, `how to
get care facebook react`, `how to react to a facebook post`). Two different subjects
  colliding on one string; neither is this page.
- **`post card` = postal.** `post card stamp`, `post card printing`, `how to address a post
card`, `post card cabins`, `post card inn` (>10K on the bare term). The reason
  `facebook post card` is only ever used with `facebook` in front of it.
- **`facebook card` = Open Graph debugger and greeting cards.** `facebook card validator`,
  `facebook card debugger`, `happy birthday facebook card`, `facebook card games`.
- **`facebook screenshot` = screenshot notifications.** The entire seed returned `facebook
screenshot notification` variants - whether Facebook tells people you screenshotted their
  story. Nothing to do with a UI screenshot.
- **Facebook-the-marketer.** `facebook post size`, `facebook post image size`, `facebook
post dimensions`, `facebook template ads`, `cover photo facebook template`, `facebook
layout size`, `how to design facebook ads`, `ai facebook post generator`. Asset

- **Old-layout nostalgia and complaints.** `old facebook ui`, `early facebook interface
2004`, `new facebook ui sucks`, `switch back to the old facebook design`, `how do i change

- **Shared social-feed vocabulary.** `social media feed react`, `social feed ui`, `social

## Notes for whoever ships this

- The claims in the FAQ were read out of the source, not the usage doc: `surface`
  post|reel|story (`FacebookFeed.tsx`), the 250-character caption cut and the
  exact/1.2K/48K/1.4M count formatting (`chrome.tsx`), the 390px/680px post widths, the
  557x878 and 1601x886 reels stages and the 486x864 story stage (`facebook-feed.css`), and
  the eight `onAction` values (`FacebookFeedAction`).
- Only `liked` and `muted` are interactive state. The two-glyph reaction cluster on the feed
  card is `role="img"` and is decorative - the FAQ says so rather than implying a reaction
  picker exists.
- The usage doc's "65px reels rail" is not a width in the CSS (the rail is `width: 0`,
  offset `right: 49px` on narrow and `55px` on wide), so no copy cites that number.
